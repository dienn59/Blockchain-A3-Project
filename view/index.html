Why Blockchain?

Tamper-Proof by Design

Every data point is cryptographically signed and timestamped on Polygon — no single party can alter the record.

🔗

Immutable Audit Trail

Each batch registration and checkpoint update is an append-only transaction, signed by the acting wallet and permanently recorded on-chain.

📱

Instant QR Verification

Consumers scan a standard QR code with any smartphone camera — no app download, no crypto wallet required. Results appear in seconds.

🛡️

Anomaly Detection

Smart-contract scan counting flags duplicate QR codes. If the same Batch ID is scanned from inconsistent locations or at unusual frequency, the consumer is alerted.

💰

Batch-Level Economics

Batch-level storage reduces transaction volume because one blockchain record can represent thousands of product units, making the system far more scalable than recording every individual unit on-chain.

🏭

Role-Based Access

Manufacturer, distributor, and retailer wallets each hold distinct on-chain roles — only authorized actors can advance a batch through the supply chain.

📊

Transparent & Public

Every record lives on a public blockchain. Regulators, journalists, or any citizen can independently verify claims without relying on corporate servers.

Process

From Factory to Your Hands

Four blockchain-backed steps ensure every product tells the truth about where it's been.

1
Batch Minted
Manufacturer registers a new batch on-chain with product name, production date, expiry, and factory location.
2
Supply Chain Updates
Each distributor or warehouse scans the batch and triggers a checkpoint event on the smart contract.
3
QR Code Linked
A QR code mapping to the Batch ID is printed on every unit. Consumers scan it with any phone camera.
4
Instant Verification
The dApp queries the blockchain and displays the full verified journey — origin, transit, expiry — in seconds.
